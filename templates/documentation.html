Start here — the one idea
1
You fill in a form

A form is only the input. If you close it without submitting, nothing is kept.

→
2
It becomes a record

On submit it is stamped with a document number, today's date and your name. That stamp is what makes it evidence.

→
3
The record joins a register

The register is the permanent list. It is never overwritten and never quietly deleted — it is the company's memory.

“The form is merely the input. The register is the memory of the company.” So a form you did not submit did not happen, and a record with no document attached is only a claim.
Your day — four habits
When you sign in
Open the bell first

Red means overdue or inside 30 days. Clear red before starting anything new — an expiry does not wait for a quiet afternoon.

{{ icon('calendar-check') }}Open the compliance calendar
Before you create anything
Search, then create

Type the client, property or agent name into the search bar. If a record exists, open it. A second record for the same thing is how commission arguments start.

{{ icon('search') }}Search every register
While you work
Record as you go

Fill the form at the moment the thing happens — during the viewing, at the end of the call. “I'll enter it later” is the commonest reason a record does not exist.

Before you leave
Attach the evidence

The signed form, the permit, the invoice. Open the record and use Attach. A typed summary is not evidence.

What is deliberately not here
Live email inbox, voice and video calls

These need a mail server and real-time messaging infrastructure that an offline, single-computer system does not have. Call logs are recorded under Sales; the call itself happens on your phone.

A separate social feed

Covered by Company Announcements under Content & Documentation, so the data lives in one place rather than being duplicated.

A generic invoices tool

Covered by Vendor Invoice Verification under Finance & Accounting, for the same reason.

An AI chatbot

The AI Insights Center is rule-based analytics — thresholds and counts over your own registers, run entirely on this computer. It sends nothing to any external service.

Where your data lives. Everything is stored in one database file on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and no external service sees your records. That also means the computer running DHARANI 360 must be backed up — if it is lost, the records are lost with it.
