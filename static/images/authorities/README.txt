Authority logos
===============

The Rules & Regulations library shows a small mark next to every issuing
authority (MOHRE, DLD, FTA, TDRA and so on). By default that mark is a
monogram DHARANI 360 draws itself: the authority's abbreviation on a coloured
tile.

Those are NOT the authorities' official logos. Government emblems and
ministry logos are protected marks, and they are not bundled with this system.

If your company holds licensed or permitted copies of an official logo, drop
the file into this folder and it will replace the monogram automatically the
next time the page loads. No code change is needed.

Naming
------
Name the file after the authority key, with a .png or .svg extension:

    mohre.png        Ministry of Human Resources & Emiratisation
    dld.png          Dubai Land Department / RERA
    rdc.png          Rental Disputes Settlement Centre
    drei.png         Dubai Real Estate Institute
    fta.png          Federal Tax Authority
    mof.png          UAE Ministry of Finance
    moet.png         Ministry of Economy & Tourism
    det.png          Dubai Department of Economy & Tourism
    fiu.png          UAE Financial Intelligence Unit (goAML)
    eocn.png         Executive Office for Control & Non-Proliferation
    cbuae.png        Central Bank of the UAE
    dataoffice.png   UAE Data Office
    tdra.png         TDRA
    difc.png         DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection
    desc.png         Digital Dubai / Dubai Electronic Security Centre
    media.png        UAE Media Council
    dha.png          Dubai Health Authority
    gdrfa.png        Residency & Foreigners Affairs / ICP
    gpssa.png        General Pension & Social Security Authority
    nafis.png        Nafis
    chambers.png     Dubai Chambers
    dda.png          Dubai Development Authority
    dm.png           Dubai Municipality
    mofa.png         Ministry of Foreign Affairs
    moj.png          Ministry of Justice
    moi.png          Public Prosecution / Ministry of Interior
    cabinet.png      UAE Cabinet

Format
------
Square, at least 96x96, transparent background, with a little padding built
into the image. The app renders it at 26-34px inside a rounded tile, so fine
detail and small type will not survive - a crest or emblem works far better
than a full horizontal wordmark.

Before you add one
------------------
Check that your intended use is permitted. Reproducing a government emblem
usually requires permission even for internal documentation, and the rules
differ between federal and Dubai government bodies. The monogram fallback is
there precisely so the system is useful without needing that permission.
